# Revisions

This repo contains public sample releases of Dovetell starter patterns.

Use this file to see what changed between public sample releases. Internal
source and packaging history are maintained separately.

## Revision Notes

- `sample-v01` is intentionally lightweight and designed for cloning or copying
  into a real repo.
- `Structure key` is reserved for a future stable fingerprint of the sample's
  file and section contract.
- Copy edits may happen without changing the structure key. Required file,
  section, or field changes should trigger a structure-key review.

## Public Safety

Samples should be safe to inspect publicly. Do not add private strategy,
customer details, credentials, internal launch notes, or unreconciled roadmap
material here.
